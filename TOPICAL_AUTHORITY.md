## Position and boundary

The site should help owners, designers, contractors, and facility teams select, survey, specify, install, accept, clean, and maintain shower enclosures. It does not provide structural/safety-glass engineering, waterproofing/plumbing design, accessibility certification, or unsupported leakage/performance claims.

## Evidence gates

- Glass/acrylic type, thickness, dimensions, edges, holes/notches, heat treatment, impact/breakage, hardware capacity, and support require current standards, fabricator/manufacturer data, and competent design.
- Water containment depends on floor slope, drain, curb, waterproofing, opening, seals, pressure, and user behaviour; do not promise “leakproof” from enclosure type alone.
- Hardware cycles, corrosion, accessibility, healthcare hygiene, coatings, cleaners, and warranties require named evidence and current project rules.
- Prices, lead times, service life, and maintenance intervals require dated terms. Chipped/cracked/moving glass requires isolation and qualified assessment.
